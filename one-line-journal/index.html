Privacy Policy

One Line Journal · Emperador Labs · Last updated: June 10, 2026

Overview

One Line Journal is a daily micro-journaling app. Your privacy matters to us: the app works without an account and your journal never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.

Data we collect

  • Journal entries are stored only on your device. We have no servers and cannot read, access, or recover your entries.
  • No personal information is requested: no name, email, account, or sign-in.

Sharing and export

When you use the export function, the app hands your journal text to the app you choose (email, messaging, etc.). That share is entirely under your control.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and coarse usage signals to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all journal entries stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date.
