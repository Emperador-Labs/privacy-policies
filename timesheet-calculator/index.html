Privacy Policy

Timesheet Calculator · Emperador Labs · Last updated: June 10, 2026

Overview

Timesheet Calculator estimates work hours, overtime and take-home pay. All calculations happen on your device: the app has no servers, no account, and never transmits the numbers you enter.

Data we collect

  • Figures you enter (hours, pay rate, state, filing status) are stored only on your device so the app remembers your settings. We have no servers and cannot read or recover them.
  • No personal information is requested: no name, email, account, or sign-in.

Sharing

If you use the share function, the app hands the estimate summary to the app you choose. That share is entirely under your control.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and coarse usage signals to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

Disclaimer

The app provides estimates for informational purposes only and is not payroll, tax, legal, or financial advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
